Spree Commerce 5.6 lets you gate prices and checkout behind a login, hiding them from guests until they request an account. The most popular use case, highlighted in this release, is a wholesale portal running alongside the retail shopping experience. Wholesale buyers get volume pricing and a quick order form for repeat purchases.
